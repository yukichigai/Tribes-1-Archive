# Mod Descriptions
A number of the less popular or unreleased mods I've created do not have much, if any, documentation explaining their intent or design. This document provides at least some basic description for each of my mods. _- Y|yukichigai_

## Shifter Advanced
This was an attempt to bring newer FPS features to Shifter, particularly dual wield weapons, alternate ammo types, and a separate Alternate Fire button. The latter would have required a special keybind in order to work, meaning this version of Shifter would not have been a "Serverside only" mod. Although development did not get very far several weapons were altered to work with alternate fire and dual wield configurations, including the Chaingun, Vulcan, Magnum, and Godhammer.

## Shifter Renegades
This is Renegades 3.5 with Shifter's Spawn Favorites, Spawn Random, and Save Profile functions added. That is not a simplification of the mod, that is **literally** all the mod is.

This mod was my cheeky attempt to "bridge the gap" between Renegades players and Shifter players. Despite the two mods having a lot in common (since Shifter is based off Renegades) many Shifter players were hesitant to play anything without Spawn Favorites, while many Renegades players disliked the more rapid pace of Shifter's gameplay.

Although this approach was simple to the point of stupidity, it was a mild success. No other servers sprung up running Shifter_Renegades, but the one server I hosted saw a moderate amount of traffic and players.

## ShifterXtreme (_a.k.a. Shifter_v1Xtreme_)
ShifterXtreme was what happened after I had a few fun nights playing Ultra Renegades and thought, "what if there was a version of this for Shifter?" I should say, this was my **first mod** made with that thought, back when I was relatively new to modding and hadn't made Shifter_v1Y yet. I started with Shifter_v1 7-30-00b and began cranking fire rates, damage numbers, and recharge speeds up to ridiculous levels, started making silly things like Nuke Turrets and Vulcan Turrets. Before long it was a coherent, if chaotic, mod.

Unfortunately it was not a _stable_ mod. Shifter_v1 7-30-00b already had stability issues, and cranking everything up to 11 didn't help. ShifterXtreme servers were known to crash multiple times per day if they were busy. Eventually after making Shifter_v1Y I understood where most of the stability issues came from, but rather than trying to fix ShifterXtreme I decided to just remake it from scratch using Shifter_v1Y as a base. That mod eventually became Shifter_v1YXtreme. For all intents and purposes Shifter_v1YXtreme is a better version of ShifterXtreme, but there is still a certain charm to the janky mess of the original.

## Shifter_v1GY
When Shifter_v1G was released it became _the_ mainstream version of Shifter. Greyflcn understood what most Shifter players wanted, and not only that was open to taking suggestions, advice, and even code from other modders. That included me, and Grey and I swapped a fair bit of code over the years. However, Grey did have a distinct vision for what he wanted in his mod and what he _didn't_ want in his mod, and some of my suggestions were rejected.

Shifter_v1GY was where I put those suggestions Grey opted not to use. In fact Shifter_v1GY was where I'd test a lot of those suggestions before sending Grey the code. Some were major, some were minor. Some examples:

 - Being able to select your favorites for your next spawn after you had died.
 - Point Defense Laser Mines being limited by how fast you could deploy them, not by requiring energy to deploy.
 - Changing the firing sound on the Boomstick to sound more like a shotgun

Eventually there were enough differences that I decided I wanted to make my own variant of Shifter from scratch, rather than making a mod of a mod of a mod (of a mod). Rather than using Shifter_v1 9-28-00 as a base like Shifter_v1G, I started making my own variant based on Shifter_v1 7-30-00b, which a number of Shifter servers and players still preferred even to v1G. Eventually that mod became Shifter_v1Y.

## Shifter_v2
One of Shifter's more contentious elements are its Weapons of Mass Destruction, i.e. the Tactical Nuke, Godhammer Launchers, Plasma Cannon, and LasCannon. Although there are numerous other weapons and methods of flinging destruction every which way, WMDs have an oversized impact on the field, whether it's against other players or deployed items. A friend of mine (ll') and I decided to try our hand at making a version of Shifter where WMDs were a little more varied, where each one had distinct strengths and weaknesses, though our changes weren't limited to only that. While we were working on it we tentatively called it Shifter_v2.

Although this mod didn't get very far, we did add a few new items. Godhammer Cannons and the Tactical Nuke were removed and replaced with Twin shoulder-mounted Vulcan Cannons and the Howitzer respectively, the latter using an unused turret shape. In addition to the Underarm Rocket Launcher pack, an Underarm Mortar Launcher pack was added. The turret selection was expanded, separating laser turrets into Indoor and Outdoor Laser Turrets, as well as adding Vulcan Turrets.

Ultimately there's not a lot to Shifter_v2 beyond a handful of extra items, though they are fairly unique.
