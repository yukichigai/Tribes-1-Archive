# Y|yukichigai's Tribes 1 File Archive
This is an archive of some of the various files (mostly mods) that I have created and collected over the years of playing and modding _Starsiege: Tribes_. 

## My previously released mods

These mods were ones I previously released publicly, but which have been made unavailable or hard to find due to various sites shutting down in the last 20 years.

 * Shifter_v1Xtreme
 * Shifter_v1Y
 * Shifter_v1YXtreme
 
## My unreleased mods

These are mods which I worked on but never publicly released. They may be buggy and/or incomplete, and are almost certainly out of date.
 
 * Shifter_advanced
 * Shifter_v1GY

## Other hard-to-find mods

These are some mods that I can't seem to locate anywhere on the web, but which I happen to have for some reason. 

 * ShifterX

## Server-specific mod variants

Finally, I have two server-specific mod variants that were never released publicly.

 * Shifter_vSiyx - From Siyx's Shifter Server, based on Shifter v1 2000-07-30
 * Shifter_FAMM - From Fallen Angels/Multi Mayhem. Shifter_v1Y with standard Shifter_v1 visuals

## My map conversions

### Find and Retrieve conversions

I converted three _Capture the Flags_ maps to _Find and Retrieve_ maps.

* Angelic Battle
* Dammit
* HiddenBroadside12
  
### Next Door conversions

Additionally, I made two versions of the map _Next Door_ to work without requiring DOX's CTF Map Pack. This is done by replacing the terrain used, and since the replacement terrain does not have gaps in the ground to allow access to the sub-level structures the maps have added teleporters, which require additional included script files to function.

* Next Door2
* Next Door3
